# Privacy Policy — kaiz0.ai Instagram assistant

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

This page describes how the Instagram automation used by the **@kaiz0.ai** account (the Meta app "kaiz0 ai manager") handles data.

## What the app does
When you comment a keyword (for example "STAGE") under a @kaiz0.ai post, the app sends you one Instagram direct message with the free resource announced in that post (a guide and ready-to-use prompts) and may post a short public reply under your comment ("check your DM"). If you reply to that message, the app can send the follow-up steps described in the conversation (for example a link once you have followed the account).

## Data the app processes
- Your Instagram username and the text and time of the comments you leave under @kaiz0.ai posts.
- The messages you send in the conversation with @kaiz0.ai and whether you follow the account (information Instagram makes available to the account owner).

The app does not ask for, and does not store, your e-mail address, phone number, password or payment information.

## How the data is used
Only to send you the resource you asked for by commenting, to avoid sending it twice, and to count how many people asked. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## Storage and retention
A record of answered comments (comment id, username, time) is kept in a private file on the account owner's computer so that a comment is never answered twice. It is deleted at the latest 90 days after the related post. Instagram itself stores the conversation under its own terms.

## Your choices
- You can block or report @kaiz0.ai on Instagram at any time; the app will no longer be able to message you.
- To have your record deleted earlier, send a DM to @kaiz0.ai with the word "DELETE".

## Contact
Instagram: **@kaiz0.ai** (direct message).

## Third parties
The app uses the Instagram Graph API provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. Meta's own data policy applies to Instagram: https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy
